Counting method

Only the two body paragraphs of each of the five cases; headings, implementation lists, introduction, and conclusions excluded.

E = paragraphs containing a literal alias (case-insensitive word boundaries); I = additional paragraphs with a documented semantic annotation and no literal alias. Counts have the same unit: paragraphs. I is an AI-assisted interpretation, not an objective word count. Unannotated implicit uses may be missed.

Aliases include plurals and abbreviations listed in the data file. Concepts are counted independently: a paragraph can contain both “prompt” and “prompt injection”. RAG does not imply use of a vector database; no such implicit link is assumed.

E: explicit paragraphs · I: implicit paragraphs. At most 2 per case and 10 overall for each term; E and I are disjoint for a given term and paragraph.
